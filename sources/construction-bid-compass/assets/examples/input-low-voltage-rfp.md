# Synthetic RFP demand — low-voltage cabling subcontract

> **Synthetic example.** All names, quantities, prices and dates are fictional. This is not a real tender and must not be used as market data.

## Demand

A fictional education campus in Sydney, New South Wales, needs a low-voltage cabling subcontract for three existing buildings. The current cabling is end-of-life and cannot support the planned classroom technology refresh.

## Facts supplied by the buyer

- **Scope:** supply and install Cat6A cabling, containment, patch panels and testing/certification for 240 outlets across three buildings.
- **Business outcome:** reliable 1 Gbps+ connectivity for classroom devices and a supported warranty path.
- **Budget range:** AUD 180,000–240,000 excluding GST; budget date 2026-10-31.
- **Required live date:** 15 March 2027.
- **Geography:** Sydney metropolitan area; work in occupied buildings with school-term constraints.
- **Incumbent:** none; a clean-sheet competitive process.
- **Evaluation panel:** facilities manager (lead), IT network lead, finance business partner, WHS adviser.
- **Response window:** 3 weeks from issue.
- **Contract form:** buyer's short-form services subcontract; no departures accepted without written qualification.

## Mandatory requirements

1. Current electrical contractor licence valid in New South Wales.
2. Public liability insurance of at least AUD 20 million.
3. Manufacturer certification/registration for the specified Cat6A system.
4. Site-specific WHS management plan and working-with-children check for site staff.
5. Ability to service the Sydney metropolitan area within 4 hours for warranty call-outs.
6. Three comparable education or occupied-building references in the last 3 years.

## Desired (scored) requirements

- Program and phasing that protects teaching continuity.
- Testing/certification package and as-built documentation quality.
- Cable and containment quality, including separation from power.
- Waste, make-good and heritage/occupied-building care.
- Commercial clarity: no hidden variation rates, clear warranty terms.
