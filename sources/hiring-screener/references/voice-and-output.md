# Voice and output rules — Floe guide adaptation

The upstream skill referred to a shared plugin voice profile. That shared document is not part of this package. Use these rules instead.

## Voice

- Use writing samples the owner supplies: past candidate emails, offer letters or a style guide.
- If no sample exists, write plainly, briefly and humanly in neutral business English (or the user's language). Say the replies are un-voiced rather than inventing a personality.
- A small business should not sound like a corporate HR department. Short sentences, a real greeting, no stock phrases.

## Output

- Produce the shortlist, drafts and checklist as Markdown files in the task workspace.
- Keep the internal shortlist (scores, evidence, notes) separate from candidate-facing messages.
- Redact or omit protected personal data from the shortlist unless the owner needs a specific field to contact the candidate. Never put ID numbers, date of birth, home address or identity-document contents in an output.
- A self-contained HTML review copy is optional; it must not depend on external scripts, fonts, images or trackers.
- This guide never sends or schedules. Every message is labelled with its intended recipient and left for the owner to send.
