net cash: its second period opens at -3,000 and receives 13,000, so it closes
at -3,000 + 13,000 − 7,000 = 3,000.

on day 65** before later receipts. A positive period-end balance does not
establish that payroll is covered. The downside reaches **-10,000 on day 35**
before AR-A arrives on day 40.

If opening cash is removed from the input, all ending-balance and payroll
coverage claims must be removed: deliver net change only. This is synthetic
arithmetic, not an accounting or collection guarantee.
