**Customer:** Northwind Analytics Ltd (customer/buyer)
**Vendor:** Helio Cloud Systems, Inc. (vendor)
**Your side:** customer/buyer

1. **Fees and payment.** Customer shall pay all invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt.
2. **Limitation of liability.** Except for Customer's payment obligations, each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the three (3) months preceding the claim.
3. **Indemnification.** Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer from and against any and all claims, losses and liabilities arising out of or relating to any breach of this Agreement.
4. **Data.** Vendor may process Customer personal data as necessary to provide the services. Vendor will use commercially reasonable security measures.
5. **Term and renewal.** The initial term is twelve (12) months. The Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
6. **Termination.** Either party may terminate for material breach after a thirty (30) day cure period.
7. **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules.
