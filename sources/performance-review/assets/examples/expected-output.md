## Calibration discussion points

1. **E3 — thin evidence.** A "Meets" rating rests on one note about attitude. Either obtain specific examples of delivered work or record the rating as provisional/insufficient evidence. Do not finalise a rating on a personality impression.
2. **E5 — recency.** All three examples are from the last six weeks. Ask for evidence from earlier in the year; if none exists, the rating is provisional.
3. **E2 — narrow evidence.** Two examples from one project do not show consistent performance across the year. Request a second context.
4. **E4 — below rating.** The two examples support the concern, but the pack does not show whether the employee received prior feedback and support. Confirm before calibration; a Below rating without that history is a process risk.
5. **Distribution shape.** The team is top- and bottom-heavy relative to the reference bands. Discuss whether the anchors are being applied consistently and whether the evidence supports the spread. Do not adjust ratings merely to match the bands.
6. **E1 — promotion case.** Strong performance, but peer leadership evidence is limited to one project. Discuss whether that gap matters for the next level and what evidence would close it.
