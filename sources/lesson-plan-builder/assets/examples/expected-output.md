# Expected output shape and acceptance notes

The response should contain a single lesson plan with:

1. Basic information for grade 8 physics, 45 minutes, one period.
2. Three objective groups (knowledge / skills / attitudes) using observable verbs, at most two per group.
3. A teaching-process table whose time column totals exactly 45 minutes. Lead-in is at most 5 minutes and opens with a phenomenon or question.
4. At least one hands-on activity naming what the teacher does, what students do and the expected output.
5. Every objective mapped to an assessment method.
6. Basic and advanced differentiated homework, 2-3 items each, with expected time.
7. A blank teaching-reflection section.
8. No invented standard code or textbook page. Because no textbook was supplied, the plan says "refer to the corresponding chapter of the textbook in use".

Acceptance checks:

- Time arithmetic is correct.
- The two named misconceptions are addressed by the activity or assessment.
- No student names or real class data appear.
- The reply does not restate the entire plan; it states assumptions, design focus and one or two adjustment points.
