# Hiring Packet

Adapted from the Apache-2.0 source in `references/PROVENANCE.md`. This guide
creates workspace drafts from supplied materials. It connects to no applicant
system, searches no private drive and routes no signature envelope.

## Establish the role

Collect the business/team, role title and purpose, reporting line, location
and working arrangement, employment type/hours, desired outcomes, actual
responsibilities and checkable must-have qualifications. Ask for the interview
stages/owners and approved prior job posts or rubrics when available.

Compensation is a supplied amount/range with currency and basis, or remains
unknown. Do not substitute “competitive” or invent market benchmarks. Do not
infer work authorization, benefits, employment terms or local compliance from
the company's name. List missing matters for the hiring owner.

Use only the supplied relevant files. If comparable public postings are
provided, identify their dates and distinguish market observations from this
employer's actual commitments. If none are available, draft from the brief
and say that no live market comparison was made.

## Write the job post

Lead with purpose and outcomes, then responsibilities, work arrangement,
essential qualifications, optional strengths, supplied compensation/benefits,
and the employer's supplied application route. Preserve an approved existing
format where practical. Make each required criterion checkable; turn vague
“strong leader” language into an observable job-related responsibility rather
than inventing a threshold. Keep required and preferred criteria distinct.

Use inclusive, job-related wording. Do not request protected or irrelevant
personal attributes, invent a legal exemption, or turn an unsupported
assumption into a candidate requirement. Keep employer policy text attributed
to the supplied policy and flag unclear terms for the responsible owner.

## Create the interview guide

For each actual interview stage, specify its owner, the competencies it covers,
four to six relevant questions, follow-up probes and role-specific **1/3/5**
scoring anchors. Assign distinct angles when two stages touch a competency;
do not repeat the same interview. Include a final evidence-based debrief.

If a resume-screening template is requested, keep it separate: list must-have
checks plus weighted preferred criteria whose weights sum to 100, with clear
0–3 evidence anchors. This package does not read, score or rank applicants and
makes no hiring decision. Never infer a candidate attribute not in evidence.

Define what a weak, adequate and strong answer demonstrates *for this role*.
A blank rubric is not evidence about an actual person. Avoid a generic
“culture fit” score that substitutes personal similarity for job requirements.

## Deliver

- Job-post draft, clearly marked with the proposed role and review date.
- Interview-stage guide, competency ownership map and scoring anchors.
- Optional blank screening template and a weights check.
- Open questions and every unsupported employer commitment left as a gap.

Use Markdown without requiring another Skill. If the user requests DOCX or
another format, use Floe's available document capability and report the file
only after the actual save succeeds. Do not claim a file was created from
text shown in chat alone. Routine workspace drafts follow the existing task
permissions and need no new approval layer.

Do not publish a job, contact a candidate, route signatures or send an offer.
Offer-letter law and candidate decisions are outside this guide. If asked to
fill a supplied approved offer template, preserve its terms and identify
unfilled administrative fields; route any external action through the App's
existing authorized flow instead of treating this guide as permission.

The synthetic intake and expected packet in `assets/examples/` illustrate
the distinction between role criteria and an actual applicant evaluation.
