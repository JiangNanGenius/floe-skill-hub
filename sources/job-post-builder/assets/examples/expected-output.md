# Synthetic expected packet

- Job-post draft names the actual location, arrangement, reporting line,
  outcomes and AUD salary range. Benefits, contribution basis and application
  route remain open questions; no market claim or employment term is invented.
- Stage 1 covers planning and stock-data evidence; stage 2 covers explaining
  changes and a reliable handoff. Questions and 1/3/5 anchors are specific to
  these responsibilities, with distinct competency ownership.
- Optional blank resume rubric separates the two supplied must-have evidence
  checks from weighted preferences: 40 + 35 + 25 = 100. It contains no real
  applicant score, ranking or hiring decision.
- Deliver Markdown drafts unless a real requested file export succeeds.
  No job was published, candidate contacted or offer sent. An offer letter
  is not invented from the absence of an approved template.

Illustrative planning anchors: 1 = gives an unsupported buy quantity;
3 = explains demand, stock and lead-time inputs; 5 = also tests arrival timing,
pack constraints and named uncertainties with an auditable calculation.
