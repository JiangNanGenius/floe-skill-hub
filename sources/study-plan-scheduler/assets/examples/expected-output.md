# Expected output shape and acceptance notes

The response should contain three tables:

1. **Weekly plan** with 8 real date ranges, phases, weekly goals, main tasks, planned hours, verifiable completion markers and status.
2. **Daily plan** with dated rows only for Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, each task taking no more than 90% of the available slot, review/catch-up included, task types mixed.
3. **Methods and rules** with spaced review, mistake review, mock-exam cadence and a concrete "if one week behind" compression rule.

Acceptance checks:

- Total planned hours do not exceed 7 hours per week; a 10-15% buffer is visible.
- The final two weeks include at least two full-length mocks, each followed by an equal review block.
- The first day of each week includes a 15-minute previous-week review.
- No score is guaranteed; the plan says the mock evidence determines readiness.
- The reply explains phase logic, time comparison and the catch-up rule, and does not restate every table cell.
