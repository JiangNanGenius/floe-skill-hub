# Estimate review checklist

Use this before an estimate leaves the workspace. Tick each item and write the evidence or `UNKNOWN`.

## Basis

- [ ] Project name, number, estimate date and revision are stated.
- [ ] Currency and tax basis (inclusive/exclusive) are stated once and do not drift.
- [ ] Measurement basis, drawing/spec revision and scale are stated.
- [ ] Location/region and escalation basis are stated.
- [ ] Allowances and exclusions are listed; no unknown scope is hidden in a percentage.

## Quantities and rates

- [ ] Every line has a non-empty description, a unit and a WBS code.
- [ ] Every quantity is greater than zero and matches the measurement basis.
- [ ] Every unit cost is zero or greater and has a source note (quote, project history, published source or synthetic example).
- [ ] No rate is an old benchmark presented as a live market rate.
- [ ] No quantity is inferred from an unscaled drawing without a warning.
- [ ] Units are consistent (for example, don't mix SF and m² in one line).

## Categories and arithmetic

- [ ] Every line uses one of labor, material, equipment, subcontractor or other.
- [ ] Direct cost equals the sum of line totals.
- [ ] Category amounts sum to direct cost.
- [ ] Overhead uses the stated base; profit and contingency use the stated running subtotal.
- [ ] Item-level markups and estimate-level markups are not double-counted.
- [ ] Rounding is stated; the grand total recomputes from the displayed amounts.
- [ ] A credit/negative line keeps its sign and is labelled.

## Presentation

- [ ] The estimate is labelled an estimate, not a tender, fixed quote or final account.
- [ ] Assumptions and `UNKNOWN` items are visible next to the total.
- [ ] The reviewer can recompute every category and markup from the written record.
- [ ] A qualified estimator/commercial owner has reviewed it before issue.
