- All 21 sources are original upstreams. The business lane records `mirror: false` for all 44 candidates; no curated list or mirror is used as a source.
- Titles and descriptions are bilingual where the lane supplied Chinese; the 44 business descriptions received an editorial Chinese paraphrase from the English purpose. Inputs/outputs remain in the language supplied by the lane.
- Public outputs contain no local snapshot, prepared-package, audit or host paths. Lane traceability is retained separately and is not part of this public artifact.
